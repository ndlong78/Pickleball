# PickleUp V4.9.0 Stable — Plan vs Actual

New section on Progress page: manual actuals versus the existing V4.7 weekly schedule.

- Current-week confirmed days / current goal, on-plan versus off-plan confirmations, and actual minutes recorded manually by the user.
- Only scheduled days that have elapsed enter the on-plan denominator; future days are not counted as missed. Unconfirmed is not skipped.
- Eight-week trend of manual V4.8 check-ins and minutes, no automatic attendance from Academy scores.
- Past weeks do **not** have historical target snapshots. Any comparison to past check-ins uses today's current goal as a **reference**, not a historical adherence percentage.
- Conservative recommendation after at least 3 of the previous 4 finished weeks have manual records. No automatic schedule change, no catch-up pressure.
- Fully read-only: no new storage keys; JSON backup schema from V4.8 remains compatible.
- Node.js 24, Playwright Chromium/WebKit regression suite, versioned PWA 4.9.0.
